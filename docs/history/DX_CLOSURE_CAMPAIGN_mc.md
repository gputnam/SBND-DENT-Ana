# dx closure campaign: intervention matrix, results and ranking (2026-06-10)

## Setup

Benchmark: the as-applied SCE field (`apparent_truth_map_mc_v2.npz`,
`delta_applied` = p − p_scecorr from truth-matched hits; dome ±0.47/0.71 cm).
Metrics: dx slope ⟨m·t⟩/⟨t·t⟩ (target 1), dx RMS, dy/dz preservation, and an
**A-variant signal-retention control** (`a_slope` = the same intervention run
on the true-arrival-time sample, which has NO hit-time bias; values well
below the 0.85 pipeline ceiling mean the intervention destroys real signal).
Tuned variants (`ridge*` λx, `scale*` k) are **calibrated on the odd-z voxel
half and validated on the even-z half** — all quoted numbers are validated.
Primary sample: the B-variant truth-hit parquet (125M hits, production
content); 18 pipeline runs (11 interventions + 7 A-controls), all with the
adopted `--anode-extrapolate` chord fix; offline ridge/scale post-processing
from per-run accumulator dumps (`rank_dx_interventions.py`,
`run_dx_campaign.sh`, outputs under `campaign/` and `campaign_results/`).

## RANKING 1 — best MC closure

The top tier is a six-way statistical tie at validated slope 0.99–1.01:
**any of {no intervention, X-only θ_xw cut, hit-time LUT} + calibrated
damping (scale k*≈0.52 or x-ridge λx*)**. Differentiators within the tie:

1. **cut 0.8 + ridge*/scale*** — best signal retention (a=0.899; the cut
   selects hits whose timing is trustworthy, and its A-control is the only
   one ABOVE the no-cut ceiling), best dy/dz at full stats. RMS 0.25–0.27.
2. **LUT + scale*** — best dx RMS (0.182) but the LUT is MC-truth-derived
   (transfer caveat) and its own A-control is the weakest of the tier
   (0.759: applying it to clean times distorts them).
3. **no intervention + scale*/ridge*** — simplest; RMS 0.20.

**Production-format cross-check** (subset, 2-iteration config,
`campaign/R{0,1}sub`): identical results — cut+ridge* 1.000/0.245,
none+scale* 0.997/0.201 — with the calibration constants STABLE across
samples and formats (k* = 0.52–0.53, λx* = 0.11 no-cut / 0.42 cut,
everywhere).

**Full-statistics validation** (existing full-sample maps, validated half):
cut 0.8 + scale* (k*=0.509): **dx slope 0.999, RMS 0.267; dy/dz slope
0.976/0.958, RMS 0.054/0.051** (8,398 voxels). No-cut production map +
scale* (k*=0.465 — different k because that map predates the chord fix):
dx 0.998/0.253, dy/dz RMS 0.087/0.080 (9,962 voxels).

### Recommended MC-closure recipe
`--anode-extrapolate --min-abs-slope 0.8` + dx scale k* (or λx* ridge)
calibrated on MC truth. Closure: dx slope 1.00 ± 0.01, RMS ~0.26 cm
against a 0.48 cm RMS field; dy/dz at their best-ever (0.05 cm RMS).

## RANKING 2 — best data-applicable (no MC calibration transferred)

**No structural intervention closes dx unaided.** The honest list:

1. cut 0.8, bare: slope 1.93 — clean but uncorrected (the ×2 remains);
2. detrend(+cut), bare: slope 0.43–0.61 with a_slope 0.45 — removes the
   artifact AND half the real signal; not usable as-is;
3. global detrend: disqualified (destroys all components).

The ×2 is generated by *track-correlated* reco-time structure: no per-hit
binned correction (1- or 2-angle LUT — identical results), no angular
selection, and no per-track de-trending can separate it from signal at the
per-voxel level. Data paths forward, in order of defensibility:
(a) transfer the MC scale k* with a quoted transfer systematic (the fake
dome is a hit-finder property, largely field-independent — but k* mixes
fake and real fractions, so it DOES depend on the field; treat with care);
(b) in-situ self-calibration from the angle-dependence of per-voxel dx
(the bias structure is measurable in data the way `angle_bias_mc` measures
it in MC — future work); (c) a joint fit with per-track time nuisances
(the structural fix; major upgrade).

## Per-intervention verdicts

* **`--anode-extrapolate`** (in all runs): real bug fix, adopt always.
* **X-only angular cut (tan θ_xw ≥ 0.8)**: does not close dx alone (1.93)
  but is the best BASE: top A-control (0.899), best dy/dz, and it feeds
  the calibrated step hits with trustworthy mean timing. Harder cut (1.2)
  is strictly worse (coverage and RMS).
* **x-ridge / scale calibration**: the closing step; equivalent to each
  other (ridge ≈ adaptive per-voxel shrinkage, scale ≈ global). On MC,
  validated closure at 1.00. The previously "arbitrary" λx≈0.1 is hereby
  given its principled meaning: it implements the ≈0.52 shrinkage that
  cancels the measured fake-to-total ratio.
* **Hit-time LUTs**: 1-angle and 2-angle are *identical* (1.858/1.860) —
  definitive evidence the residual is not parameterizable by per-hit
  angles. Mild RMS benefit when combined with scale; MC-derived.
* **Per-track detrend**: mean and linear both over-subtract (track means
  carry real field signal; a_slope 0.45 in X-only mode, ~0 globally).
  Not viable in any tested form.
* **Combinations**: no synergy found — LUT+cut, detrend+cut, detrend+LUT
  all reduce to their dominant component. The fake dome is one common-mode
  systematic, not a sum of independently removable pieces.
